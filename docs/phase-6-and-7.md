# Local expansion: Phases 6 and 7

The first five stages delivered the local model. The user authorized Phase 6 and explicitly retained SQLite. No external credentials, paid service, carrier API or hosting is required for this expansion. Phase 7 remains unstarted.

## Phase 6 — Shipping operations

Complete locally on 21 November 2026. See [validation and usage](phase-6-validation.md) for checks, review results and operating limits.

- Customer-specific manual rate cards; lowest-cost, fastest and balanced allocation using supplied estimates.
- Exact SKU-to-box capacities/weights/dimensions with destination matching and pickup warehouse allocation.
- Atomic split/merge with quantity and value conservation, cancelled source records and source/replacement links.
- Reviewable bulk simulated shipment preparation and booking with stable retry keys.
- Staff-only CSV/TSV tracking import with preview, per-row results and duplicate protection.
- International destination/postal details, HS codes, declared value/currency, purpose/incoterm and appointment requests.

## Phase 7 — Reporting, finance and usability

- Richer analytics, maps and delivery-performance reports.
- Advanced Excel reports and configurable local schedules.
- Freight-invoice imports, reconciliation and discrepancy tracking.
- Company branding, preferences and dark mode.
- Improved feedback entry and larger bulk-processing limits.
- Integrated testing, usability fixes and updated documentation.

At Phase 7 completion, these agreed features should work locally with available resources and manual inputs. Real carrier dispatch, live availability, carrier-confirmed appointments, customs filings and external notifications remain dependent on separately authorized integrations.
